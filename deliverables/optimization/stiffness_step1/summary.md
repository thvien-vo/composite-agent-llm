# Fixed thickness, stiffest laminate (objective max_stiffness): step 1

IM7/8552, simply supported 0.5 m x 0.5 m plate, Ritz double sine series 8 x 8 terms (as the fewest-plies study), shear checked for +Nxy and -Nxy, safety factor 1.5 on the strength margin min(RF Max Stress, RF Tsai-Wu) and on lambda. Objective: largest lambda; when the load cannot buckle the plate, smallest in-plane compliance c = N^T A^-1 N [N/m]. Ties (relative 1e-9): larger strength margin, then the lexicographically smallest sequence (0 < +45 < -45 < 90, from the outer ply).

When no design of N plies meets both constraints the problem is reported as INFEASIBLE (proven by the exact search), never as an optimum. The design shown is then the best one of the order: the largest strength margin when no design meets strength, else the largest lambda among the designs that meet strength. 'max strength margin' is the largest strength margin of ANY valid design of N plies.
